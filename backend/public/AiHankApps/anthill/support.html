遊戲支援

本頁適用於目前的 0.36.0 單機遊戲版本。你可以築巢、採集資源、管理蟻群並探索地表。

需要註冊帳號嗎？

本版不需要遊戲帳號，亦不提供雲端存檔。遊戲進度和設定儲存在裝置的 App 資料區。

如何調整語言與聲音？

使用遊戲內的設定控制項切換繁體中文／英文，或調整音效與環境音音量。

移除 App 會影響進度嗎？

刪除 App 或清除其資料可能使本機進度和設定遺失。重新安裝前，請先確認裝置的備份設定。

如何回報問題？

請記下 App 版本、裝置型號、系統版本，以及問題發生前的操作步驟。可選擇附上截圖，幫助說明畫面上的問題。

Game support

This page covers the current 0.36.0 offline game version. Build a nest, gather resources, manage your colony and explore the surface.

Do I need an account?

This version does not require a game account or offer cloud saves. Game progress and settings are stored in the app's data area on your device.

How do I change language or sound?

Use the in-game settings controls to choose Traditional Chinese or English and adjust sound effects and ambience volume.

Will removing the app affect my progress?

Deleting the app or clearing its data may remove local progress and settings. Check your device's backup settings before reinstalling.

How can I report a problem?

Note the app version, device model, operating system version and steps leading to the problem. Screenshots are optional and can help explain what happened.
