App 內的資料

本政策適用於 Hank Huang 開發的「螞蟻王國：築巢拓境」（Formicrown: Ant Kingdom），以目前的 0.36.0 單機遊戲版本為範圍。

遊戲進度與設定

遊戲會在裝置的 App 資料區儲存進度、語言及音量等設定，以便你繼續遊玩。本機排錯紀錄也用於檢查遊戲操作與執行狀況。

帳號與線上服務

本版不提供遊戲帳號、雲端存檔、廣告或應用程式內購買；分析與雲端當機回報服務未啟用。本版的遊戲程式沒有自動上傳遊戲存檔的功能。

管理本機資料

你可以透過裝置設定管理 App 與其本機資料。刪除 App 或清除其資料可能使進度和設定遺失。裝置備份是否另行保留資料，取決於作業系統與你的備份設定。

主動聯絡支援

你可以自行寄信至 hankhuang0516@gmail.com。我們會收到你的寄件信箱及你選擇提供的問題描述、裝置／版本資訊和附件，並用於回覆、排查及處理該問題。遊戲不會自動寄送這些資料。請勿提供密碼、驗證碼或付款資訊。

支援資料的保存與刪除

支援來信僅在處理問題及必要後續聯絡期間保留，之後刪除不再需要的內容。你可使用原寄件信箱來信申請查詢或刪除曾提供的內容；我們只會要求辨識該來信所需的最少資訊。本機存檔需由你在裝置上管理。

外部服務

你主動開啟支援／隱私網站或使用電子郵件時，網站、郵件供應商及平台會依其服務規則處理連線或來信資料。這些網站按鈕會交由裝置瀏覽器開啟，遊戲不會隨連結附上存檔或遊戲紀錄。App 商店、TestFlight 與裝置系統的處理亦依其各自政策。

政策更新與聯絡

若遊戲的資料處理方式變更，會更新本頁及相關商店聲明。隱私問題與資料刪除申請請寄至 hankhuang0516@gmail.com。

Data within the app

This policy covers Formicrown: Ant Kingdom (螞蟻王國：築巢拓境), developed by Hank Huang, and the current 0.36.0 offline game version.

Game progress and settings

The game stores progress and preferences, including language and volume, in the app's data area on your device so you can continue playing. Local diagnostic records help check gameplay and runtime behavior.

Accounts and online services

This version does not offer game accounts, cloud saves, advertising or in-app purchases. Analytics and cloud crash reporting are disabled. The game code does not include automatic uploading of game saves.

Managing local data

Use your device settings to manage the app and its local data. Deleting the app or clearing its data may remove progress and settings. Whether a device backup retains data depends on your operating system and backup settings.

Contacting support

You may choose to email hankhuang0516@gmail.com. We receive your sender address and the problem description, device/version details and attachments you choose to provide, and use them to respond to, investigate and resolve your request. The game does not automatically send this information. Do not include passwords, verification codes or payment details.

Support-data retention and deletion

Support messages are retained only while resolving your request and completing necessary follow-up, then content that is no longer needed is deleted. Email from the original sender address to request access to or deletion of information you provided. We request only the minimum information needed to identify that correspondence. You manage local game saves on your own device.

External services

When you choose to open a support/privacy website or use email, the website, mail provider and platform handle connection or correspondence information under their service policies. Website buttons open in your device browser; the game does not attach saves or gameplay records to these links. App stores, TestFlight and the device operating system also follow their own policies.

Updates and contact

If the game changes how it handles data, this page and relevant store disclosures will be updated. For privacy questions or deletion requests, email hankhuang0516@gmail.com.
