App Review 操作指南

  1. 啟動遊戲後點選「新遊戲」。不需要測試帳號、密碼或特殊設定。
  2. 在世界地圖選擇主線或支線關卡。
  3. 進入陣前準備，切換武將並拖曳角色完成移動佈陣。
  4. 進入戰鬥後，拖曳英雄設定方向與力道；放開後角色移動並攻擊。
  5. 趙雲能量充足時可施放「七進七出」，查看完整技能與連續斬殺演出。

遊戲在所有上架地區的功能與內容一致，沒有區域限定差異；不屬於醫療、金融、博弈、保險或法律等受監管服務。

English review summary

Paper Flick Soldiers is a single-player tactical flick game with no account, login, demo credentials, purchases, advertising, Google Analytics, or cross-app tracking. Android 1.0.3 and later use Firebase Crashlytics NDK to transmit crash logs, diagnostics, and an installation identifier solely for stability fixes.

To review: launch the app, choose New Game, select a main or side mission on the world map, switch and position heroes during preparation, then drag and release a hero to move and attack. The app functions consistently in every supported storefront region.
